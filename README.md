# Three-Body Universe

An interactive three-dimensional simulation with three mutually gravitating stars and four planets. Three planets start near their host stars; a fourth explores the combined gravitational field.

Drag to rotate; scroll or pinch to zoom. Click a body, follow a star or planet, adjust simulation speed, pause/resume, toggle orbital trails, reset the system or restore the camera. Camera and control preferences are saved locally. No sign-in or external assets are needed.

The simulation uses Newtonian gravity with a fixed-step velocity Verlet integrator and softened close encounters. Planets are massless test particles, stellar masses are scaled, and displayed sizes are enlarged. This is an illustrative simulation, not a reproduction of a specific astronomical system.
